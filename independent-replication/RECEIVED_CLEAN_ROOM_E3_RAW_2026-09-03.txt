counted unless they perform domain-specific work. No target vocabulary was used.

3. Independently derived semantic distinctions

The pairs require these independently variable coordinates:
(D1) continuity of the same referent versus a distinct referent;
(D2) a referent's present condition/state;
(D3) technical enabling conditions and possibility;
(D4) normative permission/legitimacy or rejection;
(D5) temporal applicability/validity interval;
(D6) occurrence/action attempt, including issuance;
(D7) externally realized effect, which may diverge from an attempt;
(D8) external fact versus an observation/report about it;
(D9) provenance/evidential support and verification;
(D10) unresolved result versus confirmed success or failure;
(D11) temporal order and partial/concurrent order;
(D12) retained causal/history structure.
D11 and D12 are structural coordinates and can be carried by generic relations
only if the relation type and its semantics are explicit and bounded.

4. Candidate basis

The smallest defensible factorisation I find is:
S1 referent continuity (a generic carrier binding/equality operator);
S2 state/condition;
S3 technical enablement/possibility;
S4 normative permission/legitimacy;
S5 temporal validity;
S6 attempt/occurrence;
S7 realized external effect;
S8 information assertion/observation;
S9 provenance/evidential support;
S10 outcome resolution (unknown, confirmed success, confirmed failure);
plus three structural operators: ST carrier association, SO temporal/causal
ordering (including partial order/concurrency), and SH retained history.

S1, ST, SO, and SH are generic structural operators, not domain primitives,
provided they do not encode hidden domain predicates. S2-S10 are semantic
coordinates. S8 and S9 are deliberately separate: identical content may have
different provenance, and provenance can change while content is fixed.
S10 is not a generic “status” bucket: its permitted values have bounded
semantics and must not absorb attempt, effect, or evidence.

5. Basis definitions

S1: the criterion that two mentions bind to one continuing referent, rather than
merely sharing an observable value. It does not assert any state or outcome.
S2: the condition of a referent at a time (open/closed, running/stopped,
usable/unusable, active/suspended, or analogous domain condition).
S3: whether technical prerequisites and resources hold, so an operation is
technically possible. It is not permission.
S4: whether a rule or authority permits, rejects, or legitimates the operation.
S5: an interval or temporal applicability validity attached to a rule,
authorization, or approval.
S6: an occurrence/attempt/issued instruction, independent of its result.
S7: a change or non-change actually realized in the external world, including
confirmed absence of the intended effect. It is not an observation of that fact.
S8: a received statement, measurement, signal, inspection, or record directed
at a proposition. It can be wrong or incomplete.
S9: the source/provenance and evidential support status of S8 (verified,
unverified, direct, or otherwise materially differentiated). This is not the
proposition's truth.
S10: epistemically resolved outcome: unresolved/unknown, confirmed success, or
confirmed failure. Confirmation requires evidence represented through S8/S9.
ST associates a record with a referent; SO represents before/after or a
partial/concurrent order; SH retains a path/history graph. These operators are
generic and inspectable, not opaque interpreter conventions.

7. Removal analysis

Remove S1/ST: Q01, Q13, and Q25 collapse same referent into coincidental equal
conditions. No concrete generic field can restore continuity without becoming
an identity primitive. FALSIFIED.
Remove S2: Q02, Q14, Q26 and the usable/unusable/resource cases lose condition
variation. Recasting condition as arbitrary metadata is laundering. FALSIFIED.
Remove S3: Q04,Q16,Q28 cannot distinguish technical impossibility from allowed
operation. S4 cannot absorb it without becoming composite. FALSIFIED.
Remove S4: Q03,Q15,Q27 lose permission/rejection while technical possibility
is fixed. S3 cannot reconstruct authority. FALSIFIED.
Remove S5: Q10,Q22,Q34 lose interval variation. A timestamp field with interval
semantics is the same accountable coordinate, not a reduction. FALSIFIED.
Remove S6: Q07,Q12,Q19,Q23,Q35 lose the fact that an attempt/command occurred.
An effect or report cannot reconstruct an attempt that had no effect. FALSIFIED.
Remove S7: Q11,Q23,Q35 collapse attempted action and external realization.
S6 is insufficient. FALSIFIED.
Remove S8: Q05,Q17,Q18,Q29,Q30 and later signals lose receipt/observation.
External fact alone cannot say what was known. FALSIFIED.
Remove S9: Q06,Q18,Q30 lose verified versus unverified support with fixed
content. S8 would have to absorb provenance. FALSIFIED.
Remove S10: Q07,Q12,Q24,Q31,Q36 collapse unknown and confirmed failure; later
evidence cannot change a representation with no resolution coordinate.
FALSIFIED.
Remove SO: sequence and concurrency in Q09,Q21,Q33 become indistinguishable.
FALSIFIED. Remove SH: Q08,Q20,Q32 lose retained path/amendment/maintenance
history while current state is fixed. FALSIFIED.

8. Merge analysis

S3+S4 cannot merge: Q03/Q04 hold one fixed while varying the other. A single
“constraint” becomes a case-dependent catch-all. FALSIFIED.
S6+S7 cannot merge: Q11,Q23,Q35 explicitly diverge. FALSIFIED.
S8+S9 cannot merge without an inspectable product type containing both
independent coordinates; that is a bundle, not one primitive. Raw merge
FALSIFIED; a typed pair is an equivalent factorisation.
S8+S10 cannot merge: a report can exist while result is unknown, and a later
report can resolve it. FALSIFIED.
S2+S7 cannot merge: a present condition can be observed, or an effect can occur
without the same representational role. FALSIFIED.
S1+S2 cannot merge: same condition with different referent identity occurs in
Q01/Q13/Q25. FALSIFIED.
S5+S4 cannot merge: the same permission has different validity intervals.
FALSIFIED.
SO+SH cannot merge generally: order is a relation over occurrences, whereas
retained path/history can differ with the same current order. FALSIFIED.
S9+S10 cannot merge: evidence quality and resolved outcome vary independently.
FALSIFIED.

9. Split analysis

“Constraint” splits into S3 and S4 because Q03/Q04 provide independent
variation. “Status” splits into S2, S10, and (where present) S8/S9; current
condition, epistemic resolution, and evidence are independently variable.
“Action result” splits into S6 and S7 by Q11/Q23/Q35. “Claim” splits into S8,
S9, and S10 because content, support, and resolution are not interchangeable.
“History” splits into SO and SH when order can remain fixed while retained path
changes. S1 is not split merely because identity has labels: no supplied pair
requires two independent identity coordinates. PASS for these justified splits;
further splitting is EXPLICITLY UNRESOLVED WITH BOUNDED REASON because the
finite corpus supplies no independent counterexample.

10. Anti-laundering analysis

Attributes, fields, tags, arbitrary JSON, opaque tokens, unrestricted strings,
or prose could encode every S1-S10, but each would carry the corresponding
independent semantic force and therefore be a hidden primitive/bundle. A
relation is acceptable only when its domain, range, direction, and bounded
meaning are explicit. “metadata=failed”, “status=whatever”, or an unrestricted
predicate is not a reduction. Generic carrier identity, order, and history
relations carry structural force but no domain-specific interpretation; if a
relation's meaning changes per case, the reduction is FALSIFIED.

11. Generic carrier analysis

Variables, equality, membership, tuples, and ordinary composition are carriers.
S1 is counted only for the semantic obligation of continuity and referent
binding; an implementation may satisfy it with ordinary identity/equality.
This is an implementation-independent representation requirement, not a
database object requirement. A carrier that merely groups two equal readings
cannot satisfy Q01/Q13/Q25. PASS for generic-carrier sufficiency with explicit
identity binding; otherwise FALSIFIED.

12. Identity / continuity analysis

Q01,Q13,Q25 are minimal counterexamples to inferring identity from equal
observable conditions. Persistence through t1/t2 requires ST plus S1. S1 does
not assert semantic sameness of condition, and S2 does not imply continuity.
Carrier identity is sufficient if it is stable across time and not a disguised
domain predicate. PASS.

13. Temporal analysis

S5 preserves applicability intervals; SO preserves order. An interval is not
merely an implementation timestamp because its bounded meaning is “valid
during”. Q10,Q22,Q34 independently vary it. Present state and historical
interval are not interchangeable. PASS for S5; global temporal ontology beyond
interval validity is EXPLICITLY UNRESOLVED WITH BOUNDED REASON.

14. Causal / concurrency analysis

SO represents before/after and partial/concurrent order without assuming a
total order. Q09,Q21,Q33 require preserving the possibility of concurrency.
SH retains a causal/maintenance/amendment path; it is a generic history graph,
not a domain primitive, but removal loses retained history. Causal direction
stronger than recorded order is not established by this corpus:
EXPLICITLY UNRESOLVED WITH BOUNDED REASON.

15. Normative / technical analysis

Q03,Q15,Q27 separate permission from possibility; Q04,Q16,Q28 separate
technical prerequisites/resources from permission. S3 and S4 therefore pass
the normative/technical criterion. “Allowed” is not a synonym for “can”.
Applicability in S5 is a temporal modifier, not permission itself. PASS.

16. Epistemic / provenance analysis

S8 records receipt/observation/report, while S9 records support/provenance.
External truth is represented by S2/S7 or another fact-bearing statement, not
by receipt alone. Q05,Q17,Q29 distinguish unobserved fact from received signal;
Q06,Q18,Q30 distinguish provenance with fixed content. S10 preserves the
resolution of an external result. PASS.

17. External-effect analysis

S6 records an attempt or issued command. S7 records realized movement,
completion, state change, or confirmed non-effect. Q11,Q23,Q35 and Q19 prevent
“attempt = effect”. A timeout alone does not establish S7 or S10 failure.
PASS.

18. Unknown / failure analysis

Unknown means no reliable resolution is represented; confirmed failure means a
reliable later signal establishes non-success. Confirmed success is likewise
distinct. Q07,Q12,Q24,Q31,Q36 require this transition to remain visible. S10
must not be inferred from absence of a signal. PASS.

19. Heterogeneous counterexamples

Physical: a valve command is issued, the valve remains closed, and telemetry is
absent; later a calibrated inspection confirms non-movement. Transaction: a
payment is technically issuable and permitted, but funds are unavailable.
Contract: an authorization is valid only in a stated interval; a later
authorization has the same text but another interval. Document: two identical
signed PDFs have different verified provenance. Service: two replicas report
the same health but are distinct instances and have different restart paths.
Distributed system: two messages are delivered with no justified total order.
Approval: an approval is sent, processing times out, and a later trusted record
confirms rejection. Machine: identical current condition follows different
retained maintenance paths. These adversarial cases preserve the same
distinctions and do not require domain-specific names. PASS.

20. Alternative bases

Alternative A: one universal “constraint/status/claim/action” element plus
fields. It covers by arbitrary field conventions but fails atomicity,
anti-laundering, merge, and removal: independently variable coordinates are
hidden. FALSIFIED.
Alternative B: fact, event, relation, and evidence, with no separate
technical/normative or unknown/result coordinates. It loses Q03/Q04 and
Q07/Q12 unless those coordinates are hidden in composites. FALSIFIED.
Alternative C: the present factorisation with S8+S9 represented as a typed
evidence pair and ST/SO/SH as generic relations. It is bidirectionally
reconstructible with the listed basis and is materially equivalent. PASS.
Alternative D: split every label into domain-specific primitives (payment,
machine, contract, etc.). It generalizes poorly and adds no independent force.
FALSIFIED as a smaller-defensibility candidate.
No finite-corpus proof distinguishes all possible equivalent notations:
EXPLICITLY UNRESOLVED WITH BOUNDED REASON.

21. Strongest arguments against own basis

The strongest objection is that S10 may be derived from S8+S9 plus a
domain-independent rule for entailment. That works only if “reliable later
signal establishes failure” is itself a bounded semantic operator; otherwise
the entailment convention hides S10. I retain S10 because Q07/Q12 require a
state transition from unresolved to confirmed failure and the contract
prohibits opaque interpreter conventions.
S7 may be reducible to S2 plus SO if every effect is defined as a state change,
but Q23/Q35 distinguish an attempted external act from its result and do not
require the result to be represented as merely the next sampled condition.
S8 and S9 may be bundled as one evidence object, but that is composite, not
atomic. Identity may be generic equality rather than S1; this changes the
counting layer, not the obligation. Hidden assumptions remain around truth
conditions of reports and the granularity of history. PASS for bounded basis;
global minimality EXPLICITLY UNRESOLVED WITH BOUNDED REASON.

22. Finite-corpus / generalization risk

Apparent minimality can be an artifact: the corpus has no independent examples
of revocation versus expiry, graded confidence, conflicting reports, identity
merger, branching causality, or effect magnitude. A new case with same outcome
but different confidence could split S9/S10; a case with two simultaneous
effects could split S7. Additional evidence needed is a frozen heterogeneous
corpus varying each proposed coordinate while holding the others fixed, plus
conflict, branching, and persistence cases. No global minimum is claimed.
EXPLICITLY UNRESOLVED WITH BOUNDED REASON.

23. Implementation-independence analysis

The basis does not depend on databases, event loops, queues, APIs, agent
frameworks, object models, or execution engines. Those can implement carriers,
relations, and records. Their presence does not prove semantic primitiveness.
Conversely, replacing S3/S4/S7/S10 with software fields does not remove their
semantic obligations. PASS.

24. Unresolved questions

The corpus does not settle whether truth, reliability, and confirmation should
be separate beyond S8/S9/S10; whether causal order must be stronger than
recorded order; whether history is a first-class graph or a reconstructible
closure; or whether interval endpoints need open/closed boundary semantics.
These are explicitly bounded unresolved questions, not missing closure states.

25. Bounded conclusion

For the declared corpus and contract, S1-S10 plus generic carrier, ordering,
and retained-history operators are the smallest defensible factorisation I can
justify. The result passes coverage, removal, merge/split, anti-laundering,
temporal/concurrency, epistemic, normative, external-effect, identity, and
implementation-independence checks. Atomicity is PASS for the stated
coordinates, with S8+S9 and structural operators treated as typed/composite
where appropriate. Corpus-generalization risk is EXPLICITLY UNRESOLVED WITH
BOUNDED REASON. This is not a globally minimal ontology claim.
